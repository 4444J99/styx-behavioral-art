# Telos: Idealized Form

## Strategic Vision

`styx-behavioral-art` serves as the creative derivation engine within the **ORGANVM** pipeline, translating formal behavioral-economics theory from **ORGAN-I** (`organvm-i-theoria/styx-behavioral-economics-theory`) into visual language, generative systems, and performance scores for downstream consumption by **ORGAN-III** (`organvm-iii-ergon/peer-audited--behavioral-blockchain`).

## Theoretical Grounding

1. **First Genuine I→II Derivation:**
   Consumes mathematical models of stake mechanics, audit cycles, commitment contracts, and behavioral game theory, elevating raw protocol data into expressive aesthetic artifacts.

2. **Core Concept Pillars:**
   - **Stake & Commitment Visualization:** Force-directed graphs and horizon skylines translating stake weight, duration, and audit resolutions into living data art.
   - **Audit Cycle Generative Art:** L-system growth trees and cellular automata tapestries driven by audit cycle rhythms (challenge, respond, resolve).
   - **Accountability as Performance:** Reframing the public ledger as a theatrical score and audit events as dramatic beats.

3. **Downstream Value:**
   Provides public-facing visual identity, onboarding narrative metaphors, and interactive frontend interfaces for the behavioral blockchain product.
